This policy covers RushTrail Runaway, including RushTrail DEV for Android (com.antigravity.rushtrailrunaway.dev). This development build is intended for players aged 13 and over.

Progress and settings

Your coins, gems, heroes, worlds, gifts, chest rewards and settings are stored on your device. Your optional RushTrail nickname is also stored on this device, separately from game progress. It is editable in Profile and is not uploaded or made public in this build; rankings are not connected yet. Reward receipts help prevent duplicate grants. Android backup or device transfer may include this data if enabled on your device. This build has no game sign-in, developer-operated account database, cloud-save service or separate analytics SDK.

Age selection

The age slider has no preselected age. After you confirm, only an age range (under 13, 13–15, 16–17 or 18 and over) is saved on your device. The exact selected number and a date of birth are not saved or sent to a developer server. Players reporting under 13 cannot enter the game. Unknown-age and under-13 players cannot request ads. Android backup may include the saved age range.

Ads and consent

This build uses Google AdMob test ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for applicable ad privacy choices. After an eligible age choice, opening Shop may request consent information and prepare an optional rewarded Common Chest ad. A reward is granted only when Google's SDK reports that it was earned. No real-money purchase or forced-ad placement is active in this build.

Google says its Mobile Ads SDK can automatically collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), app and ad interactions, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers. These are used for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. The SDK encrypts its data in transit. Local gameplay does not mean that Google's services collect no information.

Ad requests use Google's teen treatment for players under 18 and a G maximum ad-content rating. For UMP consent messages we conservatively treat players under 16 as under the age of consent. Ads remain unavailable when UMP does not permit requests. If Google requires a privacy-options entry, Settings and Shop show an ad privacy control to reopen its form. Consent choices do not prevent offline play.

Read Google's Privacy Policy, Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures and how Google uses information from partner apps.

Purchases, sign-in and cloud saves

The Legendary Pack is a preview. Google Play Billing, game sign-in and cloud progress saving are not active in this build. The policy will be reviewed before these features are introduced.

Retention and your choices

On-device data stays until it is overwritten, removed with the app, or cleared through Android settings, subject to Android backup and restore. Removing app data can erase progress. Google retains information under its own policies. There is no separate RushTrail account to delete in this build.

You can play offline after age selection. Ads, consent updates and external website links need internet. When you open the in-game policy online, it checks the published GitHub Pages version for the latest text. The last successfully loaded policy is saved on your device for offline reading; a bundled copy is available if no saved version exists. No game update is needed for published policy-text changes. For information held by Google, you can use Google Account privacy controls.

Security and this policy website

We use the app's device storage for local progress. Google states that its Mobile Ads SDK encrypts its data in transit. No storage or transmission system can guarantee absolute security.

The public version of this policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, including visitors who are not signed into GitHub. Online requests from the in-game policy reader also go to GitHub Pages, without sending your age, game progress or login cookies. This policy page does not add analytics scripts, advertising, external fonts or a contact form. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.
